What is Steam Roulette?

Steam Roulette is a game-picking ritual: instead of choosing yourself, you let chance decide. The name comes from Funhaus' old YouTube show — but their wheel picked from the whole Steam catalog. Ours spins over your list: your backlog, your co-op night options, your "games I keep meaning to finish".

Why Steam Roulette?

  • Built for games — every entry auto-matches to Steam: live prices, discounts and covers on the wheel itself.
  • Fair pick — cryptographic RNG (crypto.getRandomValues), every game has an equal 1/N chance.
  • Share it — one short link carries your whole wheel. Send it to your co-op group and let it pick tonight's game.
  • Presets & elimination — multiple saved lists, hide games you're not in the mood for, remove winners until the list is done.
  • No sign-up — everything stays in your browser. Free and open source (MIT).

How it works

1. Add games — one by one, pasted in bulk, or auto-linked to Steam.
2. Spin — the wheel decelerates with a soft tick and picks your game.
3. Play — or hit "Remove winner" and let it burn through the backlog.

FAQ

Is PileSpin free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, no ads. Open source (MIT).

How is it different from wheelofnames?

Generic wheels just pick a name. PileSpin is built for games: Steam prices, discounts and covers on every sector, presets and elimination mode.

Can I share my wheel?

Yes — one short link saves your list and settings; friends open it and spin the same wheel.

Does it read my Steam library?

Not yet — you add games yourself (bulk paste works). Library import is on the roadmap.

Is it really random?

Yes — crypto.getRandomValues, equal sector for every game. No weighting, no tricks.
